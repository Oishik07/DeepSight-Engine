**Challenges and Limitations**

- **Scalability:** Quantum computing is still in its early stages, and scalability remains a significant challenge.
- **Ethical Decision-Making:** Agentic AI systems will need clear boundaries for ethical decision-making to ensure that they support, rather than replace, human intelligence.
- **Cold Start Problem:** The cold start problem is a significant challenge in the adoption of quantum computing, where the initial investment required to get started is high.

**Technical Details**

- **Shor's Quantum Algorithm:** Shor's quantum algorithm is a polynomial-time algorithm for the efficient factorization of large integer numbers into prime factors.
- **Quantum Workflow Management:** AI agents can bring autonomy and intelligence to quantum workflow management, enabling the efficient operation of quantum systems.
