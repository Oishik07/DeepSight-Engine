**Jeevan Akshay VI Pension Plan**

**Overview**

Jeevan Akshay VI is a pension plan offered by LIC India, a leading life insurance company in India. The plan provides a guaranteed income for life, with various annuity options to choose from.

**Annuity Options**

According to the policybazaar.com website, Jeevan Akshay VI offers the following annuity options:

1. Annuity payable for life at a uniform rate
2. Annuity payable for 5, 10, 15 or 20 years certain and thereafter as long as the annuitant is alive
3. Annuity for life with return of purchase price on death of the annuitant
4. Annuity payable for life increasing at a simple rate of 3% p.a.
5. Annuity for life with a provision of 50% of the annuity payable to spouse during his/her lifetime on death of the annuitant
6. Annuity for life with a provision of 100% of the annuity payable to spouse during his/her lifetime on death of the annuitant
7. Annuity for life with a provision of 100% of the annuity payable to spouse during his/her lifetime on death of the annuitant. The purchase price will be returned on the death of last survivor.

**Returns**

According to the PrimeInvestor website, the effective returns from Jeevan Akshay VI work out to anywhere between 5.21% pa and 6.71% pa, depending on the option chosen.

**Taxation**

The PrimeInvestor website also mentions that if the purchase price is opted to be returned to the nominee, the amount will be taxable in the hands of the receiver.

**Mutual Fund SWP**

**Overview**

Mutual Fund SWP is a popular investment option for generating income in retirement. It allows investors to invest in a mutual fund and withdraw a fixed amount of money at regular intervals.

**Returns**

According to the Groww website, the returns from Mutual Fund SWP can range from 8% to 12% pa, depending on the investment option chosen.

**Taxation**

The Groww website also mentions that the returns from Mutual Fund SWP are taxable in the hands of the investor.
